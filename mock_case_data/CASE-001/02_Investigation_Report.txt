Summary of Investigation:
1. Statement of complainant Ramesh Kumar recorded on 03-Feb-2026, confirming the
   sequence of the phishing call and OTP disclosure.
2. Call Detail Record (CDR) for the number used to contact the complainant
   (+91-7XXXXXXXX4) was requisitioned from the telecom provider. The number was
   found to be registered on a fake ID and was active for only 6 days before the
   incident, consistent with a "burner SIM" pattern.
3. Bank Transaction Report obtained from Trustline Bank (see Document 03) shows
   three transfers to a mule account (A/C No. YYYYYYYY7790) held in the name of
   Ajay Verma at Trustline Bank, Rajpur Branch.
4. Ajay Verma was traced through the mule account KYC details and detained for
   questioning on 15-Feb-2026. His statement is recorded separately (Document 04).
5. A mobile phone and a laptop were seized from Ajay Verma's residence at
   45 Nehru Nagar, Rajpur, under seizure memo SM/2026/0091. Forensic imaging of
   the seized devices is documented in the Evidence Report (Document 05).
6. Preliminary findings indicate Ajay Verma acted as a "mule account operator" for
   a larger, yet-unidentified vishing syndicate; funds were withdrawn in cash
   within 2 hours of credit and the trail beyond that point could not be traced
   through banking channels alone.
