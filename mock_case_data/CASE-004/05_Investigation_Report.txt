INVESTIGATION REPORT
Case No: MP/2026/00212
Investigating Officer: Inspector Lata Menon
Report Date: 25-May-2026

Summary of Investigation:
1. FIR and detailed Missing Person Report registered on complaint of Mahesh
   Sharma regarding his daughter Kavita Sharma (Documents 01, 02).
2. Witness statement of classmate Ritu Nair (Document 03) indicated Kavita had
   been in contact with an unidentified online acquaintance referred to as
   "Sam" shortly before her disappearance.
3. CDR analysis (Document 04) confirmed the missing person's last known
   location as Rajpur Bus Stand at 13:02 hrs on 11-May-2026, following a call
   to an unregistered prepaid number activated shortly before the incident.
4. CCTV footage from Rajpur Bus Stand for the relevant time window has been
   requisitioned and is under review; preliminary review shows a female
   matching Kavita Sharma's description boarding an intercity bus at
   13:10 hrs, seated next to an unidentified male.
5. The bus operator has been contacted to identify the destination and any
   passenger manifest details for the 13:10 hrs service; response awaited.
6. Description and case details have been circulated to neighbouring district
   police stations per standard missing-person protocol.

Cross-References: FIR MP/2026/00212; Missing Person Report MPR/2026/0212;
Witness Statement (Ritu Nair); CDR Summary CDR/2026/0212.

Status: Active investigation. Priority lead is identification of "Sam" and the
destination of the 13:10 hrs intercity bus service from Rajpur Bus Stand.

Signature: Inspector Lata Menon
