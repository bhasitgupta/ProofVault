CCTV ANALYSIS REPORT
Reference No: CCTV/2026/0089
Case No: TH/2026/00089
Prepared by: Sub-Inspector Vikram Rao, with technical assistance from District
  Cyber Cell
Date: 15-Mar-2026

Sources Reviewed:
  1. Lake View Colony main gate camera (footage 20:00-22:00 hrs, 10-Mar-2026)
  2. ABC General Store camera, 200m from Colony main gate (same time window)

Findings:
- Main gate camera (Cam-1) recorded a male individual matching the description
  given by witness Suresh Babu entering the colony on foot at 20:47 hrs and
  exiting at 21:38 hrs, consistent with the estimated time of the burglary.
- ABC General Store camera (Cam-2) captured a clearer frontal view of the same
  individual at 21:41 hrs. Facial features partially visible; jacket matches
  witness description.
- The individual was subsequently identified through local intelligence sources
  as Rakesh Yadav, previously known to police in an unrelated case of
  house-breaking in the neighbouring Sunrise Colony (2024).
- Still frames extracted and hash-sealed as Exhibit EX/2026/0089-CCTV1 and
  EX/2026/0089-CCTV2.

Conclusion: CCTV evidence corroborates witness statement and identifies Rakesh
Yadav as the primary suspect. Recommend suspect be traced and detained for
questioning.

Signature: Sub-Inspector Vikram Rao
